Voice-driven mobile money

Speak your language. Bank on your own.

Kasa turns a normal phone call into a full MoMo session — check balance, send money, buy airtime, hear a statement — in Twi, Ewe, Ga or Dagbani. No app, no menu, no English required.

Try the call

Tap start, then just talk. Kasa listens and answers out loud, in whichever language you choose — nothing here is typed or read on screen.

Needs a mic and a browser with speech support, like Chrome — and your sound on.

How a call moves through the system

Four layers, one existing rail underneath: MTN's MoMo platform.

TelephonyAnswers the call, streams audio both ways
↓
Language & AI LayerSpeech → intent → speech, per language
↓
Application BackendHolds context, enforces confirm‑before‑acting
↓
MoMo PlatformExecutes the transfer, top‑up, or balance pull

What launches, what waits

In scope

  • Balance, send money, airtime, mini statement
  • Twi, Ewe, Ga, Dagbani
  • Mandatory spoken confirmation before any transfer
  • Re-prompt flow when speech isn't understood
  • Monitoring console for live oversight

Not yet

  • Loans, credit, or savings products
  • Any screen-based interface
  • Merchant or business accounts
  • Cross-border transfers
  • Execution without a spoken "yes"
